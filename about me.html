about me

I’m a Web Developer and Cybersecurity Specialist with over five years of experience creating responsive, secure, and high-performing websites. My passion lies in building modern digital experiences that not only look great but are also protected from potential threats. I specialize in front-end development, WordPress customization, and UI/UX optimization, always aiming to deliver clean, efficient, and user-friendly results.

Currently, I’m completing a Mitacs Globalink Research Internship at Carleton University (CyberSEA Lab), where I’m developing web-based tools for cybersecurity impact analysis. My work involves designing a knowledge graph for Industrial Control Systems (ICS) security, building web scrapers for research automation, and visualizing complex cybersecurity data in intuitive web interfaces.

I have strong technical skills in HTML5, CSS/SCSS, JavaScript, Python, and MySQL, along with practical experience applying secure coding practices, digital signatures (RSA, DSA, ECDSA), and network traffic analysis using tools like Wireshark. This combination of web development and cybersecurity expertise allows me to create reliable, high-quality solutions that balance performance, usability, and security.

I’m a proactive learner who thrives on solving complex problems and exploring new technologies. Whether working independently or in a team, I focus on delivering value through innovation, attention to detail, and a strong commitment to continuous improvement. I’m open to new opportunities, collaborations, and freelance projects that allow me to grow and make a real impact.
